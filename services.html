Good to know

Service questions.

What information should I send for a quote?

Send your suburb, photos of the rubble or garage, a short description of the material and any access challenges. This helps us assess the load and labour needed.

Do you clean garages as well as remove rubble?

Yes. Garage cleaning is one of our services. Share photos of the space and explain what needs to be cleared so we can quote for the load and work involved.

Can you quote for more than one load?

Yes. Let us know roughly how much material you have and send clear photos. We’ll discuss the likely number of loads and confirm pricing for the job.

Which areas do you cover?

We serve Johannesburg townships, Sandton, Randburg, Midrand, Bryanston, Kempton Park, Melrose, Edenvale, Bedfordview, Fourways, Kyalami, Rivonia and Sunninghill. Nearby? Ask us to confirm.
