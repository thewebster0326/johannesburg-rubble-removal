Pricing FAQs

Before you book.

Is R1,200 a fixed price for every load?

No. R1,200 is the lower end of our typical range. Your quote depends on the ease or difficulty of the job and the labour required. Most jobs fall between R1,200 and R1,800 per load.

How do I get an exact price?

Call or WhatsApp us with your suburb, photos of the load and details about access. We’ll assess the particular job and confirm the quote with you.

What if my cleanup needs more than one load?

Tell us what you can see and share wide photos of the full pile or space. We’ll discuss the likely number of loads and the total job price before booking.

Does garage cleaning cost the same as rubble removal?

Garage cleaning is assessed individually because the amount of clutter, sorting and labour can vary. Send photos so we can provide a job-specific quote.
